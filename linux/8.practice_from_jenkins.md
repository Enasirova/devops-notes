# ls -ldh vs du -sh

ls output: size of directory record itself: `ls -ldh /var/lib/jenkins/plugins`

output:
`drwxr-xr-x 234 jenkins jenkins 36K May 13 14:43 /var/lib/jenkins/plugins`


du output: space of the folder and everythign inside it: `sudo du -sh /var/lib/jenkins/plugins` /va
